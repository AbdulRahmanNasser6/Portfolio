CV WEBSITE - HOW TO PUBLISH
===========================

1. Upload EVERYTHING in this folder to your server, keeping the same structure
   (index.html, style.css, script.js, three.min.js, fonts/, the two PDFs, og-image.png, favicon.svg).
   No build step and no internet dependency: fonts and 3D library are included.

2. Replace the placeholder address with your real one (find and replace):
      https://YOUR-DOMAIN
   Files: index.html (5 places), sitemap.xml, robots.txt

3. "View project" buttons: open index.html, find each  href="#"  inside a card,
   and put your project link there. Until you do, the button shows "Project link coming soon".

4. Terminal: press the ` key (or Ctrl+K) or click "Terminal". Type  help.

5. Language: the site opens in Arabic or English according to the visitor's browser.
   Force one with  ?lang=ar  or  ?lang=en  at the end of the address.

6. Share preview (WhatsApp / LinkedIn): after step 2 the og-image.png is used automatically.
   If a platform shows an old preview, refresh it with that platform's link debugger.
